Economy

An export-led year: merchandise exports of $475.04bn against imports of $455.01bn, a goods surplus of $20.03bn, and realised foreign investment at a five-year high. All 2025 trade figures are preliminary NSO releases.

V2 · Trade & investment, 2025

Merchandise trade is F.O.B. for exports and C.I.F. for imports. The services balance ran a deficit of $10.23bn, so the combined goods-and-services balance is roughly +$9.8bn.

Source: NSO, 2025 preliminary. Realised FDI $27.62bn, +9.0% year on year — the highest in five years. Hover or tap a bar for the exact value.
