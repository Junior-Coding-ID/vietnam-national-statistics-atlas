Viet Nam in numbers — 2025

One atlas, two honest series. The headline figures are the National Statistics Office of Viet Nam's own; flip the switch to see the World Bank's version of the same year, and read the reconciliation note before you quote a provincial total.

Headline series: NSO Official (National Statistics Office of Viet Nam)

V1 · Growth held, prices stayed calm

Real GDP growth against CPI inflation, 2015–2025. The pandemic trough (2020–21) and the 2022 rebound are the story of the decade; 2025 is the joint-highest growth year of the series at 8.02%.

Source: NSO real GDP growth; CPI annual average, NSO / World Bank WDI (FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG). 2022 first published as 8.02%, later revised to 8.12%. 2025 growth is a preliminary estimate. Hover or tap any bar or point for the exact value.

V5 · A $500-billion economy crosses $5,000 per person

Nominal GDP in current US dollars and GDP per capita, 2015–2025. The economy more than doubled in ten years; 2025 is the first year GDP per capita passes $5,000 on the World Bank series.

Source: World Bank WDI, GDP nominal (NY.GDP.MKTP.CD); GDP per capita is GDP divided by UN World Population Prospects 2024 population. NSO's own 2025 figures are $514.0bn and $5,026 per capita (VND 12,847.6 trillion) — switch the headline series above to see them.

2025 in brief

  • Growth 8.02% — the fastest since the 2022 rebound, on construction (+9.62%) and a strong export year.
  • Trade surplus $20.03bn on merchandise exports of $475.04bn; realised FDI hit a five-year high of $27.62bn.
  • 63 provinces became 34 on 1 July 2025 (Resolution 202/2025/QH15). Ho Chi Minh City is now 14.00 million people.
  • Fertility fell to 1.66 on NSO civil registration — well below replacement level 2.1, and below the UN estimate of 1.88.
  • Unemployment 2.22% on NSO's working-age measure; the ILO-modelled figure is 1.52% — different definitions, both shown on People.
