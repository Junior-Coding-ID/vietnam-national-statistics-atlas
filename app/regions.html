Regions — from 63 to 34

On 1 July 2025 Resolution 202/2025/QH15 cut Viet Nam's provincial-level units from 63 to 34: 28 provinces and 6 centrally governed cities. Twenty-three of the 34 are new mergers; 11 kept their old borders. Tap any tile to jump to its row in the table.

V3 · The new map — tile cartogram

A schematic cartogram: each tile is one province or city, placed roughly where it sits on the map (north at the top). It is not a geographic map — tile size is equal so small dense provinces stay readable.

  • Merged in 2025 — 23 units formed from 52 former provinces and cities.
  • Unchanged — 11 units kept their borders: Hanoi, Huế, Lai Châu, Điện Biên, Sơn La, Lạng Sơn, Quảng Ninh, Thanh Hóa, Nghệ An, Hà Tĩnh, Cao Bằng.
  • Gold dot — centrally governed city (6: Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Haiphong, Da Nang, Huế, Cần Thơ).

The biggest new unit is Lâm Đồng (24,233 km², from Lâm Đồng + Đắk Nông + Bình Thuận). The smallest is Hưng Yên (2,515 km²). The most populous is Ho Chi Minh City at 14.00 million; the least is Lai Châu at 0.51 million.

Source: Resolution 202/2025/QH15 (National Assembly, 12 June 2025; effective 1 July 2025). Populations are the resolution's administrative-registration figures — see the reconciliation note at the top of this page.
